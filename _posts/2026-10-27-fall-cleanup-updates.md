## Feasibility Study Update
​The Feasibility Study contract has finally been signed from Jim Feeney, Arlington’s Town Manager. The town has contracted with [KZLA](https://www.kylezick.com/) to perform the feasibility study. The order of milestones is as follows, though we don't have a timeline or updates for when each step is completed. Please keep an eye out particularly for the public input survey which should happen in the next 2 months.

- Base plan development from GIS
- KZLA site visit
- Draft public input survey
- Close comment period
- Conceptual design
- **Public input survey**
- Close comment period
- Complete study

At the completion of the study, the town should have an idea of range of cost and scope of the work. Before the commission or any community members can advocate for the town to budget for this playground rebuild, a feasibility study must be completed.
